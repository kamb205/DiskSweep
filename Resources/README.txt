DiskSweep — read me first
=========================

DiskSweep cleans junk off your Mac and helps it run faster.
Made with ♥ by Kamran · 2026

What you need
-------------
• macOS Tahoe (26) or newer.
• Any Mac that runs Tahoe: Apple silicon (M1 and newer) or a supported Intel Mac.

Install
-------
1. Drag DiskSweep onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click DiskSweep.

"Apple could not verify DiskSweep…"
-----------------------------------
DiskSweep isn't sold through Apple, so the first time you open it macOS blocks it.
This happens once:

1. Click Done on the message.
2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the message about DiskSweep and click Open Anyway.
4. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway again.

Full Disk Access
----------------
To look at your whole disk, DiskSweep needs Full Disk Access. The start screen
walks you through it: click Open Privacy Settings, switch DiskSweep on, then
click Relaunch.

Is it safe?
-----------
• Nothing is deleted outright. Everything you remove goes to the Trash, so you
  can put it back until you empty the Trash.
• Your documents, photos, mail, messages, passwords, iCloud files and macOS
  itself are never offered for removal.
• Items marked "Review" or "Careful" are never ticked for you. Look before you remove them.
• Speed Settings only reads your settings. You switch things yourself.
