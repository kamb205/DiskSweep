# Changelog

## 1.0 (October 2026)

First public release.

- **Smart Care**: one-click selection of junk that's safe to remove.
- **Speed**: Memory, Free Up Memory, auto-quit for inactive apps, Background Items, Speed Settings checklist.
- **Cleanup**: System Junk, Developer Junk, AI Models, Installers, Downloads, Trash Bins.
- **Files**: Large Files, Old Files, Duplicates, Space Lens, Music & Stems.
- **Applications**: Uninstaller, App Leftovers.
- Menu bar panel, keyboard shortcuts (⌘1–⌘9), universal app for Apple silicon and Intel on macOS 26 Tahoe.
